Welcome guide for Ambuhub referrers

Thank you for inviting someone to Ambuhub. This short guide covers how clients use dispatch, marketplace services, and your referral rewards.

What Ambuhub helps with

  • Request ground ambulance dispatch when you need urgent transport.
  • Browse hire, book, and sale listings from verified providers.
  • Track orders, favorites, and notifications from your client dashboard.

Using dispatch safely

Ambuhub connects you with available units, but it is not a replacement for local emergency numbers when someone needs immediate professional medical care.

Sharing Ambuhub

Keep sharing your personal referral link from Client → Referral. Every successful client signup helps grow safer medical logistics access in your community.
